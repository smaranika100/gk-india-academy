1. Introduction & Core Commitment

Welcome to GK India Academy (accessible at https://gkindiaacademy.com). We operate as a free, open-access competitive examination preparation initiative dedicated to assisting aspirants across India preparing for UPSC, SSC, Banking, Railway, Defence, and State Public Service Commission examinations.

We are committed to operating in full accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023 of India, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and international privacy principles.

Student-First Privacy Pledge

GK India Academy does NOT require compulsory registration to study GK topics or solve practice MCQs. You can browse all study notes, check notifications, and practice tests 100% anonymously. We never sell or monetise personal student contact data.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect minimal technical and usage data strictly required to deliver and optimize our services:

  • Directly Provided Information: When you voluntarily reach out via our contact channels or optional feedback forms, we receive your name, email address, and message content.
  • Local Browser Storage (Client-Side Only): To deliver seamless exam practice, your browser stores quiz scores, saved bookmarks, solved question state, and UI theme preferences in HTML5 localStorage. This data resides exclusively on your local device and is never uploaded to third-party databases without your consent.
  • Automated Analytics & Log Information: Standard server logs record anonymous technical metadata such as browser type, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, and timestamps to assist in server debugging and DDoS defense.

3. How We Use Collected Information

The information we collect is utilized strictly for the following purposes:

  1. To display and format educational materials, exam notices, and MCQ practice test sessions.
  2. To diagnose website latency, ensure responsive mobile rendering, and maintain 99.9% portal uptime.
  3. To respond promptly to student questions, feedback, or corrections submitted for question explanations.
  4. To safeguard the integrity of our platform against malicious automated scrapers or cyber security attacks.

4. Cookies & Local Tracking Technologies

GK India Academy uses minimal, strictly essential cookies and local storage tokens for performance optimization. For comprehensive information on how cookies function and instructions on how to disable them in your browser, please review our dedicated Cookie Policy.

5. Third-Party Services & Embedded Media

To provide high-quality learning materials, we integrate selected trustworthy external resources:

  • Google Fonts & CDN Resources: We utilize Google Fonts and Cloudflare CDN to deliver crisp typography and fast asset loading globally.
  • YouTube Video Embeds: Selected current affairs and topic classes embed YouTube video lectures hosted on official educational channels. Viewing embeds is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase Cloud Infrastructure: Our backend administration database is secured via Supabase with enterprise Row-Level Security (RLS) and encrypted connections.

6. Data Security Measures

We employ industry-standard HTTPS encryption (TLS 1.3), Content Security Policies, and automated vulnerability scanning to protect data in transit. Although no Internet transmission is 100% immune from compromise, we continually test and audit our code to prevent unauthorized access.

7. Student Rights Under Indian Law (DPDP Act 2023)

As a user of our portal, you are entitled to the following rights regarding any data concerning you:

  • Right to Access: You may request confirmation of any data retained about your interactions.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: You can clear your local study bookmarks and progress at any time directly through your browser settings or by asking our team to purge your feedback messages.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Inquiries submitted to our designated grievance officer will be addressed within 30 days.
