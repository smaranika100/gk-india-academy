1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by your web browser when visiting websites. HTML5 LocalStorage is a modern browser feature that allows websites to store small amounts of non-sensitive data locally on your phone or computer.

At GK India Academy, we rely primarily on client-side local storage so that you can bookmark important questions, review exam progress, and retain filters without requiring personal account registration or server logins.

2. Categories of Storage We Use

We classify our browser storage into three clear categories:

Storage Key / Type Category Duration Purpose
gk_cookie_consent Strictly Necessary 1 Year Stores your cookie banner preference (accepted or declined non-essential features).
gk_saved_bookmarks Functional Preference Persistent Stores question IDs and study notes you bookmark for revision on your device.
gk_mcq_progress Functional Preference Persistent Tracks your solved questions, streaks, and correct answer tally during practice.
sb-*-auth-token Administrative Session Session / 7 Days Strictly for verified staff administrators accessing the backend dashboard.

No Cross-Site Behavioral Tracking

We do NOT use third-party advertising cookies that follow your browsing habits across different websites. Your quiz progress and bookmark history remain private on your device.

3. Third-Party Embedded Technologies

Certain educational pages contain embedded widgets provided by third-party services:

  • YouTube Video Player: When you play embedded video lessons on our Videos section, YouTube may set cookies to monitor bandwidth, remember playback time, and measure view counts under Google's Cookie Policy.
  • Cloudflare / CDN: Utilized for DDOS mitigation and rapid worldwide asset delivery.

4. How to Manage or Disable Cookies in Your Browser

You have full control over cookie permissions through your web browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block third-party cookies or delete all stored data upon closing the browser.

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data.

Note: Disabling local storage will prevent the website from saving your question bookmarks across browser sessions, but all learning materials and MCQs will remain freely readable.
